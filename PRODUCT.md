# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Drivers, cyclists, and walkers who want to understand the time cost of choosing a route with lower historical crash exposure. They use RouteRisk in a time-sensitive, mobile context and need a clear recommendation without technical interpretation.

## Product Purpose

RouteRisk compares route alternatives by travel time and historical crash exposure. Success means a person can immediately understand what extra travel time may avoid historically elevated crash exposure, while never mistaking the estimate for a safety guarantee.

## Brand Personality

Calm, analytical, credible. The product communicates with measured confidence and explains its evidence without alarmism.

## Anti-references

Emergency alert styling, red-heavy interfaces, cartoon road imagery, generic AI gradients, cluttered hackathon dashboards, and unsupported safety claims.

## Design Principles

1. Make the time-versus-exposure tradeoff legible at a glance.
2. Explain every recommendation with human-readable evidence.
3. Use familiar navigation patterns so the route decision stays in focus.
4. Treat exposure as historical and relative, never as a promise of safety.
5. Make the demo reliable without network dependence.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support Dynamic Type, VoiceOver, light and dark appearance, large touch targets, and color-independent exposure labels. The interface must remain understandable for people with color-vision differences.
